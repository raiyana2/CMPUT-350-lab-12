Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
The boxes move and resize to enclose the same groups of circles. When circles
that started close together move apart, their group's box can get larger and
overlap other boxes. That can make the BVH check more branches for a sample.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
The tree groups circles again using their current positions. The boxes tend to
be tighter with less overlap, but they can jump when the grouping changes.
Rebuilding takes more work per frame because it has to find new splits.

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
The starter code places circle centers anywhere on the screen, including less
than one radius from an edge. The part of a circle outside the image has no
pixels to draw into, so it looks cut off.
